The hidden cost of a homegrown portal

Most teams treat the portal as a sunk cost: the build was budgeted and the license is paid. The guide quantifies what that view overlooks: four compounding failures, the FTEs required for manual upkeep, and an AI‑readiness gap that widens each quarter the portal remains in use.

THE FRAMEWORK

The criteria that actually decide it

Decide whether your estate becomes the governed system of record that grounds every AI decision, or remains a directory of links AI can’t use. The guide outlines seven criteria that distinguish a true system of record from a portal masquerading as one, letting you evaluate any option on the table.
