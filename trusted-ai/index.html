THE CONTEXT GAP

AI can reach your reports.
Does it have the context to choose the right one?

Access to analytics does not by itself establish which KPI definition or report fits a question. When definitions, ownership and exceptions are undocumented or disconnected, copilots and agents can return conflicting or poorly grounded answers.

01 / CONFLICTING ANSWERS

Two assistants give two numbers for one KPI.

Finance and Sales each publish a net sales report. An assistant that can reach both picks one and doesn’t say which.

02 / CONTEXT GAPS

The definition lives in someone’s head.

Business rules, owners and exceptions sit with the people who built the report. AI fills the gap with a best guess.

03 / TRACEABILITY

Nobody can say where the answer came from.

When a leader questions a number, the team can’t point to the certified report or definition behind it.

What we aim to map together

A business question and the KPIs it depends on; available definitions, owners and source reports; gaps in certification, freshness and access context; and a prioritized list of follow-up validation steps. Findings depend on the information available and the agreed scope.

What to bring

Bring one representative question, the BI and AI tools involved, and any definitions or sample reports you can discuss. Include a business metric owner and a BI or analytics representative where possible. Agree access, session timing, deliverables and any fees before the workshop.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Here is what teams
ask first.

Do we need Atlas or Nexus in place first?+

You don’t. The workshop works with your current BI tools and definitions, and shows what context exists today and what is missing.

Does Nexus read our raw data?+

Nexus is designed to use BI metadata and governed business context. We confirm the information accessed, permissions and deployment requirements for your use case before implementation.

How does this relate to MCP?+

MCP gives agents a standard way to reach your BI tools. It doesn’t decide which KPI definition applies to a question. The Beyond MCP whitepaper covers that gap.

Which AI tools does this apply to?+

The discussion applies to copilots and agents that answer analytics questions. We review your BI platforms, AI tools and integration requirements before confirming a suitable approach.

Does this cover AI actions as well as answers?+

If your use case includes actions, we can discuss how Maestro could support governed workflows, human review and traceability. The initial workshop focuses on the context needed for analytics answers.
